# OneCielAI Logo Concepts

Brand name: OneCielAI

Legal/company name: One Ciel LLC

Core meaning: one sky, one shared horizon, many AI projects under one brand.
`Ciel` suggests sky or heaven, so these concepts explore orbit, horizon,
constellation, cloud, compass, and multi-project systems.

## Concepts

1. **One Sky Orbit** - Best YouTube/channel avatar candidate. Compact `1C`
   monogram with sky/orbit motion.
2. **Ciel Horizon** - Best for intro cards and brand story. Emphasizes sky,
   sunrise, and a new horizon.
3. **Constellation Gate** - Good for developer/agent projects. Suggests many
   connected agents under one portal.
4. **Cloud Circuit** - Good for cloud, infrastructure, and AI automation
   products.
5. **Celestial Compass** - Good for advisory, planning, navigation, and
   orchestration products.
6. **Minimal Wordmark** - Best for banners, thumbnails, documents, and GitHub
   social preview cards.
7. **Project Constellation** - A portfolio-system direction for the upcoming
   group of around 10 projects.
8. **Infinite Ciel** - Good for long-running agents, continuity, memory, and
   connected workflows.

## Suggested Brand System

- Channel avatar: `concept-01-one-sky-orbit`
- YouTube/GitHub banner wordmark: `concept-06-minimal-sky-wordmark`
- Developer platform visuals: `concept-03-constellation-gate`
- Multi-project announcement visuals: `concept-07-project-constellation`

Use `OneCielAI` as the public brand and `One Ciel LLC` for legal, copyright,
package ownership, and footer credit.
