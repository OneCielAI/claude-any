## YouTube Spec Notes

- Profile image: PNG/JPG/GIF/BMP, no animated GIF, 15 MB or smaller, renders at
  98 x 98 px.
- Banner image: 2560 x 1440 px recommended, 2048 x 1152 px minimum, 6 MB or
  smaller. Keep text and logos inside the central safe area.
- Video watermark: square image, at least 150 x 150 px, less than 1 MB.
